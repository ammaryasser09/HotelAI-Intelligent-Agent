NILE PEARL HOTEL
PAYMENT POLICY

1. Payment Requirements

Payment requirements depend on the selected reservation and applicable hotel policies.

2. Reservation Payment

Guests may be required to provide payment information when making or confirming a reservation.

3. Payment Verification

Payment information may need to be verified during the reservation process.

4. Additional Charges

Additional charges may apply for certain hotel services or damages to hotel property.

Examples of services with specified prices include:

- Breakfast: 450 EGP per person
- Airport Transfer: 800 EGP per trip
- Laundry: 200 EGP per kilogram
- Spa Treatment: 1,200 EGP per session

5. Payment Assistance

Guests can contact customer service for assistance with payment-related questions.

6. Important Note

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify:

- A general deposit amount
- A universal prepayment requirement
- Accepted payment cards
- Payment deadlines
- Currency conversion rules
