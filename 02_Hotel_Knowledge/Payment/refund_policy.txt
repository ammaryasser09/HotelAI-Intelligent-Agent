NILE PEARL HOTEL
REFUND POLICY

1. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the conditions associated with the reservation.

2. Cancellation Refunds

If a cancelled reservation is eligible for a refund, the refund is processed according to the applicable payment and refund conditions.

3. Refund Amount

The refund amount depends on the reservation conditions and any applicable cancellation terms.

4. Refund Processing

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify a fixed refund processing time.

Guests should contact customer service for information regarding the status of a refund.

5. Refund Assistance

Guests should provide their reservation details when contacting customer service about a refund.

6. Important Note

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify:

- A universal refund period
- A fixed refund processing time
- A universal refund percentage
- Specific refund rules for every booking type
