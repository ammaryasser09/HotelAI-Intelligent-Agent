NILE PEARL HOTEL
CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Cancellation Requests

Guests may request cancellation of their hotel reservations.

Cancellation requests are subject to the applicable reservation and cancellation conditions.


2. Cancellation Eligibility

Cancellation eligibility depends on the conditions associated with the reservation.

Guests should review the cancellation conditions before requesting cancellation.


3. Cancellation Fees

Any applicable cancellation fees depend on the cancellation conditions of the reservation.

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify a universal cancellation fee.


4. Cancellation Deadline

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify a universal cancellation deadline.

Guests should check the cancellation conditions associated with their specific reservation.


5. Non-Refundable Reservations

If a reservation is marked as non-refundable, the applicable refund conditions may differ from standard reservations.

The exact non-refundable conditions should be checked before cancellation.


6. Cancellation Assistance

Guests can contact customer service for assistance with cancellation requests.

Guests should provide their reservation details when requesting cancellation.


7. Refund After Cancellation

If a reservation is eligible for a refund, the refund is processed according to the hotel's payment and refund policy.

Refund eligibility and processing conditions depend on the reservation.


8. Important Note

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify:

- A universal cancellation deadline
- A universal cancellation fee
- A fixed refund processing time
- Specific cancellation rules for every room or booking type

The applicable conditions should be checked for each reservation.
