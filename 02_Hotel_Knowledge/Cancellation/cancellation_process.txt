NILE PEARL HOTEL
CANCELLATION PROCESS

1. Identify the Reservation

The guest should provide the relevant reservation details when requesting cancellation.


2. Review Cancellation Conditions

The cancellation conditions associated with the reservation should be checked before processing the cancellation.


3. Submit Cancellation Request

The guest can contact customer service to request cancellation assistance.


4. Check Cancellation Eligibility

The reservation should be checked to determine whether cancellation is permitted and whether any fees apply.


5. Process Cancellation

If the cancellation request meets the applicable conditions, the reservation can proceed through the cancellation process.


6. Refund

If the reservation is eligible for a refund, the refund is processed according to the hotel's payment and refund policy.


7. Confirmation

The guest should retain the cancellation confirmation or relevant reservation information for future reference.


8. Important Note

The exact cancellation procedure may depend on the booking type and reservation conditions.
