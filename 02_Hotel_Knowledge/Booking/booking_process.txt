NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING PROCESS

1. Select Dates

The guest provides the desired check-in and check-out dates.


2. Select Room

The guest selects a suitable room type based on availability and guest capacity.


3. Provide Guest Information

The guest provides the required personal and contact information.


4. Review Reservation

The reservation details should be reviewed before confirmation.

The guest should verify:

- Guest name
- Number of guests
- Check-in date
- Check-out date
- Room type
- Booking details


5. Payment

Payment requirements depend on the hotel's payment policy and the selected reservation.


6. Confirmation

After completing the reservation process, the guest should receive or retain the reservation confirmation details.


7. Changes or Cancellation

Guests should contact customer service regarding reservation changes or cancellation requests.

Changes and cancellations are subject to the applicable hotel policies.
