NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING MODIFICATION POLICY

1. Reservation Changes

Guests may contact customer service regarding changes to their reservations.

Reservation changes are subject to availability and hotel approval.


2. Changes to Check-in or Check-out Dates

Guests may request changes to their check-in or check-out dates.

The requested dates must be checked for room availability.

Any applicable cancellation or payment conditions depend on the hotel's relevant policies.


3. Changes to Room Type

Guests may request a change to their selected room type.

Room changes are subject to room availability.


4. Changes to Number of Guests

Guests may request to change the number of guests on a reservation.

The selected room must have sufficient capacity for the updated number of guests.


5. Special Requests

Guests may contact customer service regarding special requests.

Special requests are subject to availability and hotel approval.


6. Modification Assistance

Guests can contact customer service for assistance with reservation changes.

Guests should provide their existing reservation details when requesting a modification.


7. Important Note

The hotel knowledge base does not currently specify:

- A modification deadline
- Modification fees
- The exact procedure for online modifications
- Whether all booking types can be modified
- Specific rules for changing guest names
