NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING CONFIRMATION

1. Reservation Confirmation

A reservation should be confirmed after the required booking information has been provided and the reservation process has been completed.

Guests should keep their booking confirmation details for future reference.


2. Confirmation Information

A booking confirmation should be associated with the reservation details provided during the booking process.

Relevant reservation information may include:

- Guest name
- Number of guests
- Check-in date
- Check-out date
- Room type
- Reservation details


3. Confirmation Assistance

Guests can contact customer service if they have questions regarding their reservation or booking confirmation.


4. Important Note

The current hotel knowledge base does not specify:

- A specific confirmation delivery method
- Email confirmation details
- SMS confirmation details
- A specific confirmation number format
- The exact time required to receive confirmation
