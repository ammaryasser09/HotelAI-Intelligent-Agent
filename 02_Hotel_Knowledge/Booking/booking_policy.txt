NILE PEARL HOTEL
BOOKING POLICY

1. Room Reservations

Guests can make reservations for available rooms at Nile Pearl Hotel.

Room availability depends on the selected dates and room type.

Guests should provide accurate booking information when making a reservation.


2. Required Booking Information

A reservation may require the following information:

- Guest full name
- Number of guests
- Check-in date
- Check-out date
- Selected room type
- Contact information
- Payment information when required


3. Room Selection

Guests can select from the available room types.

Available room types include:

- Standard Room
- Deluxe Room
- Superior Room
- Family Room
- Executive Room

Room availability is subject to the selected dates.


4. Guest Capacity

The number of guests must comply with the capacity of the selected room.

Standard Room:
Maximum 2 guests.

Deluxe Room:
Maximum 2 guests.

Superior Room:
Maximum 3 guests.

Family Room:
Maximum 4 guests.

Executive Room:
Capacity information is not specified in the current hotel knowledge base.


5. Booking Confirmation

A reservation should be confirmed after the required booking information has been provided and the reservation process has been completed.

Guests should keep their booking confirmation details for future reference.


6. Special Requests

Guests may contact customer service regarding special requests.

Special requests are subject to availability and hotel approval.


7. Booking Assistance

Guests can contact customer service for assistance with:

- New reservations
- Booking questions
- Reservation changes
- General booking information
